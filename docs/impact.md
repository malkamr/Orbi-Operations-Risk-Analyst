# Impact

## Business Value

Orbi is designed to create value across five operational areas.

### 1. Earlier Risk Detection

Identify emerging operational issues before they become customer-facing disruptions.

### 2. Faster Decision Support

Reduce the manual effort required to connect sales, inventory, order, supplier, and replenishment signals.

### 3. Prioritized Attention

Help operations teams distinguish critical risks from lower-priority watch signals.

### 4. Preventive Action

Surface recommended actions while there is still time to intervene.

### 5. Consistent Monitoring

Provide a repeatable risk-scanning workflow instead of relying entirely on manual review.

## Demonstration Impact

In the synthetic retail scenario, Orbi analyzed:

- 10 SKUs
- 6 suppliers
- 8 customer orders
- 6 inbound purchase orders
- 6 observed sales days

It identified:

- 1 Critical risk
- 3 High risks
- 2 Medium/watchlist signals

The strongest example was P003 — Tomato Sauce, where inventory, unfulfilled orders, delayed replenishment, and supplier reliability combined into a critical operational risk.

## What These Numbers Mean

These figures demonstrate the agent's ability to analyze and prioritize a controlled scenario.

They are **not claims about real-world business performance**.

No claims are made here about:

- revenue improvement
- cost reduction
- percentage efficiency gains
- reduced stockouts in production
- ROI

Those outcomes would require deployment with real business data and measurement over time.

## Success Measures for a Real Deployment

A production evaluation could measure:

| Metric | Example Measurement |
|---|---|
| Risk detection lead time | How early a material risk is identified |
| False-positive rate | How many alerts require no action |
| Risk resolution time | Time from detection to human resolution |
| Fulfillment impact | Change in late/unfulfilled orders |
| Stockout frequency | Change in stockout events |
| Replenishment reliability | Improvement in on-time inbound supply |
| Operator effort | Manual review time saved |

## Core Value Proposition

> The value is not simply finding problems.  
> It is finding the important problems early enough for people to act.
